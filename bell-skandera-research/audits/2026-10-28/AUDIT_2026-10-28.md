## Stronger exact corner lemma for all 74 archived N=2 rows

Write F(t)=prod_i(1+lambda_i*t), N=u_d=2, a_j=u_(d-j), and n=a1, m=a2. The scaled reciprocal roots beta_i=2/lambda_i have elementary symmetric coefficients e1=n, e2=2m, e3=4a3, e4=8a4. Their power sums are

    p0=d, p1=n, p2=n^2-4m,
    p3=n^3-6mn+12a3,
    p4=n^4-8mn^2+8m^2+16n*a3-32a4.

Let H2=det[[p0,p1],[p1,p2]] and H3=det(p_(i+j))_(0<=i,j<=2). Direct expansion yields

    H2 = d*(n^2-4m)-n^2,
    partial H3/partial a4 = -32*H2,
    partial H3/partial a3 = -8*S(a3),
    S(a3)=36*d*a3-10*d*m*n+d*n^3+12*m*n-n^3.

The derivative S'(a3)=36d is positive. Newton/Maclaurin log-concavity on the *reversed* coefficient sequence a_j=u_(d-j), with a_d=u_0=1, yields a3 >= L3(m) and a4 >= L4(a3) >= L4(L3(m)), where

    L_j(x)=ceil[ C(d,j)*(x/C(d,j-1))^((d-j)/(d-j+1)) ].

For each of the 74 archived exceptional rows, exact integer evaluation proves simultaneously

    H2>0, S(L3(m))>0, H3(d,n,m,L3(m),L4(L3(m)))<0.

Therefore H3 is strictly decreasing in a4 and, since S(a3)>0 for all a3>=L3(m), strictly decreasing in a3 throughout the admissible region. Thus

    H3(a3,a4) <= H3(L3(m),L4(L3(m))) < 0,

contradicting the necessary positive-semidefinite Hankel condition. **No a3 upper bound, integer quadratic maximization, or exhaustive (a3,a4) grid is needed for this branch.** This is a finite, case-dependent monotonicity proof; it does not establish a universal sign rule for all coefficient inputs.
