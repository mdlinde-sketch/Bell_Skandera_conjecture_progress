## Lemma and certificate

For a positive-root integer polynomial `F(t)=prod(1+lambda_i t)` with norm `u_d=2`, write `a_j=u_(d-j)`. The special adjacent coefficients in the d14–17 frontier fix `a1=n, a2=m`. Normalized Newton–Maclaurin inequalities give

`L3(m) <= a3 <= U3(m,n)`, and `a4 >= L4(a3) >= L4(L3(m))`,

where the exact lower chord and local upper are

`L_j(x)=ceil((C(d,j)^(d-j+1)*x^(d-j)/C(d,j-1)^(d-j))^(1/(d-j+1)))`,
`U_j(x,y)=floor(C(d,j)*C(d,j-2)*x^2/(C(d,j-1)^2*y))`.

For scaled reciprocal roots `beta_i=2/lambda_i`, the elementary symmetric coefficients are `(e1,e2,e3,e4)=(n,2m,4a3,8a4)`. Newton's identities give `p0=d, p1=n, p2=n^2-4m, p3=n*p2-2mn+12a3, p4=n*p3-2m*p2+4na3-32a4`. A necessary condition for all roots beta_i>0 is nonnegativity of the 3×3 Hankel determinant `H3=det((p_(i+j))_(0<=i,j<=2))`.

Let `H2=d*p2-n^2`. Direct symbolic expansion yields **slope of H3 in a4 = -32 H2** and **quadratic coefficient of H3 in a3 = -144d**. Every special case has H2>0. Therefore all admissible `(a3,a4)` satisfy

`H3(a3,a4) <= H3(a3,L4(L3(m)))`.

The right side is a concave quadratic in a3. Check its exact integer maximum on the entire interval `[L3(m),U3(m,n)]` using the two endpoints and the two integers adjacent to its vertex. If this maximum is negative, the entire two-dimensional coefficient rectangle is excluded. **No enumeration of individual a3/a4 pairs is needed.**
