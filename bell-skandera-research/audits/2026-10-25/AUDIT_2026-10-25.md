## What this does not establish

These checks strengthen finite arithmetic coverage for the induction base, but they use the same mathematical reduction lemmas as the prior work. In particular, Smyth's trace theorem and factor induction, KK finite cutoff, coefficient rectangle bounds, inverse trace, Hankel PSD necessity and H4 interval domination still require independent mathematical/formal review. No degree-18 attempt was made. See the 25 October source programs, reproduction guide and complete dated ZIP.
